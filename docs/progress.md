# Progress in Racinage Free

Progress in Racinage Free is private, offline-only analytics. The **Manage > Progress** section shows local score, levels, weekly consistency, badges, and Background, Subtle, or Expressive display settings.

Trusted local additions can qualify a week and earn capped score. Navigation, login, search, deletion, imports, repeated identifiers, automated work, and unreviewed plugin activity do not earn score. Duplicate or over-cap candidates are suppressed and never backfilled.

Racinage Free has no public leaderboard, alias, Canopy Points, reward redemption, plan boosts, hosted AI credits, or Progress synchronisation. All Progress rows remain in the private SQLite database under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Racinage Free`; hosted services and companion jobs cannot access it.

See [`desktop/RacinageFree/PROGRESS.md`](../desktop/RacinageFree/PROGRESS.md) for the local event catalogue and data boundary.
